## Reflection: Week 56

---

### The Mistakes We Made

#### 1. Numbers Changed and Changed Back

The round restart threshold moved from 110k to 260k tokens and back to 110k within the same day (`e90e491618`, `47dd89c6a4`). Earlier, the per-person `av_lag` shift and a brand-name look-alike spam rule were each added and reverted within hours (`b59dcef69f`, `e2ea8412cf`, `2b926e3f62`).\
**Lesson Learned:** A tuned value without a measurement behind it gets reverted. Gather the number first.

#### 2. A Refactor Deleted a Button Handler

The supplies-triage Approve and Dismiss handler vanished in an earlier commit and was restored on Nov 9 (`4822ea594e`, #1626).\
**Lesson Learned:** Deleting code that looks unused needs a search for button IDs, not just function names. Buttons call handlers by string.

#### 3. A Busy Check Matched Itself

`social-heard` skipped Instagram on every run because its "is Helium busy?" check found the job's own shell (`a6c130dae3`).\
**Lesson Learned:** A guard needs a test that runs it from inside the job it guards.

#### 4. Slow Cuts of the Same Video

Each IH20 and Traditions video took five to twenty versions. Many fixes were one word or one frame.\
**Lesson Learned:** Notes that go through a screening round with a watch link cost less than notes retyped into chat. The screening room itself is the answer to this one.

### Lessons for Building AI Systems

- **Ownership is a field.** The people table stores whose contact each person is. Prose parsing guessed wrong.
- **One config, many readers.** `config/mailboxes.php` replaced four hand-copied lists.
- **Dry-run, sweep, then retire.** `last_contact` left once nothing referenced it.
- **Give failure its own words.** "Hit an error" and "No change" replaced a hollow "Done".
- **Legal moves live in a table.** Four status systems moved to transition tables.
