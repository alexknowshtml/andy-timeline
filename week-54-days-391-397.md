## Reflection: Week 54

---

### The Mistakes We Made

#### 1. Kit Tag Import Into Mailroom Added and Reverted Same Day

A bulk Kit-tag-to-Mailroom opt-out import (`c0dd8c1f3b`) was added and reverted on Oct 30 (`5cc138ce25`). The proper path: Kit opt-outs are marked through the Kit system as people click, not batch-imported from a tag.

**Fix:** Reverted same day.\
**Lesson Learned:** Opt-outs represent individual choices. Importing them in bulk from an external tag overwrites that signal with a categorization.

#### 2. CoS Quiet Hold Skip Added and Reverted (Oct 31)

A CoS change to skip Alex's quiet hold when the turn needs nothing was added and reverted the same day. The quiet hold is load-bearing for Alex's turn rhythm.

**Fix:** Reverted immediately.\
**Lesson Learned:** The quiet hold isn't optional overhead — it's part of the pacing Alex relies on. Changes to CoS pacing require deliberate testing, not incidental removal.
