## Reflection: Week 15

---

### The Mistakes We Made

#### 1. Knowledge Base Path References Were Everywhere

**Cost:** After renaming `articles/` to `reading/` and `code-snippets/` to `patterns/`, 12 stale references across agents, SOPs, skills, and commands needed updating
**Fix:** Systematic grep and replace across the full codebase, plus automated audit to catch future drift
**Lesson Learned:** Directory renames have a blast radius proportional to how many systems reference those paths. Automated auditing catches what manual review misses.

#### 2. Email Label ID vs Name Confusion

**Cost:** Using label names instead of IDs risked creating duplicate Gmail labels
**Fix:** Hardcoded Label_495 ID for the Andy label; added safety rules to email-modify-protocol
**Lesson Learned:** Always use immutable identifiers (IDs) over mutable names when interacting with APIs. Names change; IDs don't.

---

### The Surprising Things

#### 1. 73 Bookmarks Had Never Been Filed

**Expected:** Most bookmarks would already have standalone files
**Actual:** 73 out of 148 bookmarks existed only as entries in bookmarks.md

The knowledge base restructure revealed that nearly half the curated content was trapped in a monolithic list. Breaking them into standalone files with proper categorization made them discoverable.

#### 2. Voice Streaming Latency Drop Was Dramatic

**Expected:** Incremental improvement from SSE streaming
**Actual:** 5-8 seconds down to ~1-2 seconds (75% reduction)

The batch-to-stream architecture change had an outsized impact because voice conversations are uniquely sensitive to silence gaps.

#### 3. Untrusted Content Defense Was Surprisingly Compact

**Expected:** ACIP v1.3 adaptation would be large and expensive token-wise
**Actual:** Trimmed to ~1200 tokens by removing coverage Claude handles natively

Most prompt injection defense is redundant with Claude's built-in safety. The include only needs to cover the gaps specific to Andy's architecture.
