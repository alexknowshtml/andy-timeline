## Reflection: Week 14

---

### The Mistakes We Made

#### 1. LinkedIn Skill Iteration Churn

**Cost:** 6 versions in one day, each addressing issues missed in the previous
**Fix:** Each version improved on specific pain points (lazy loading, viewport capture, folder organization)
**Lesson Learned:** Unknown platforms require rapid iteration. The churn was necessary learning, not wasted effort.

#### 2. Beads Dashboard Naming Confusion

**Cost:** Three names in one week (/beads-dashboard -> /open-tasks -> /beads)
**Fix:** Settled on /beads - short, memorable, no confusion with Andy Core tasks
**Lesson Learned:** Name commands for what users will type, not what the feature does. Shorter is better.

---

### The Surprising Things

#### 1. Image Tiling Dramatically Improved OCR

**Expected:** Full-image OCR would work for whiteboards
**Actual:** Tiling into 4x6 grids gave much better results per tile

Claude vision performs better on focused regions. Large images lose detail that matters for handwriting.

#### 2. Discord Buttons Changed Interaction Patterns

**Expected:** Buttons as convenience feature
**Actual:** Fundamental shift in how issues are managed

One-click actions from notifications creates a different workflow than typing commands.

#### 3. andy@indyhall.org Immediate Adoption

**Expected:** Experimental feature to test over time
**Actual:** Member inquiries started flowing immediately

Real demand existed for AI-assisted email responses to common member questions.
