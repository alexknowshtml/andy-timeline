## Day 382: The CoS Architecture Overhaul (October 18, 2026)

### Mid-Turn Relay: Messages Find a Running Thread (GH #1061)

**Commits `bf37664b2e`, `acf4a10c3f`, `7ce82719e5`, `4f531a0c62`, `a5a666e628`, `2ce6155f13`, `e1b245e3e5`, `950be5318e`, `1d858dd4bd`, `58c0610de8`, `ae32b2a967`, `03c4cb829b`**

You rewired how cross-thread messages reach a busy CoS. Previously, a message for the CoS while Claude was mid-turn in another thread waited in a queue. Now it's delivered directly into the running thread's inbox and read at the next tool call. The host-bridge (`bf37664b2e`) posts into a thread's running Claude inbox; every thread now launches with `crossSessionInbound=accept` by default.

Visual tracking was wired in: a relay post gets ✅ once read, 👀 when it becomes the next turn. When a relay arrives mid-turn, the CoS gets context on how to rank it against the current work. The mid-turn notice posts as the CoS persona, anchored below the relay post it describes. A small-text line records whether the turn actually read it. `cos:relay --pending` lists a thread's waiting relays; `--withdraw` removes them. The queue remains as a backstop for true concurrency — the mid-turn inbox read is the primary route.

The inbox stop hook was added (`587a113e26`), tested against real relays, and same-day reverted (`953e5dfce7`) — a late relay naturally waits about 10 seconds for the next turn, making the stop block unnecessary complexity.

### Set-Aside Replaces Parked (GH #1057)

**Commits `45e04c7e63`, `e4594de436`, `69073a3d33`, `bbe7253abf`, `46755cfc70`, `b7be1bd144`**

The Parked thread concept was retired. Set-aside now handles everything Parked did, with a cleaner contract: a set-aside card yields to a newer question and comes back once the queue is empty. Before the sweep, the thread's accumulated backlog is written to its GitHub issue as a checklist. `cos:defer` lets the CoS ask-card a thread by hand. A reposted set-aside card reads as "asked twice" with a fresh timestamp rather than repeating identically. The brought-back card carries the original turn's buttons. `CosParker` was stripped to only its closing marks, close path, and legacy-row compatibility — everything else gone.

### Thread Rename + Close-Check Foundation (GH #1059)

**Commits `debb8c349c`, `10d5e572ae`**

Thread renames now clear the cached name and retitle all open cards. A brand-new thread has no name cache — it builds fresh. When a thread gets new work after a close offer, the once-per-thread rule resets so the next clean check posts a fresh card rather than skipping.

### Open Items Candidates + Verdicts (GH #1060, #1062, #1065, #1066)

**Commits `dd41416ba0`, `287d399d0c`, `2a60728c76`, `5edecf5bf2`, `ed87fc88d7`, `ae1aea4926`, `05b017eac6`, `83efd4e805`, `5e88a85b51`, `86cf02ffd6`, `8a35c79d22`, `419e86774d`**

`snapshot.ts` was split into per-command modules — each command owns its snapshot logic. Every caveat Andy wrote in a thread now becomes an open item automatically, not just explicit TODOs. Digest.py was extended to require an explicit in/out verdict for every flagged digest line before the run saves; quiet runs, handoffs, and failures blamed on other work are listed too. Cut-reply lines that hold open or caveat cues are preserved. Each run's snapshot stores the candidate verdicts; delta reads carry the last full run's verdicts so follow-on reads don't lose context. `replay-cut.py` cuts a thread dump at a snapshot's cursor for testing. Candidates now flag test-gap notes and Alex's questions. The close check (`419e86774d`) was updated to blame the last writer of a shared file and ask the host whether a hash shipped, rather than assuming.

### Thread Intent System (GH #1077)

**Commits `cdcb002954`, `28d605af02`**

New threads now declare intent at creation: **build** (Claude is producing an artifact), **file_only** (storing information without back-and-forth), or **ask** (a question needing a response). When `startIntent` is `build`, the new-thread flow drops the Continue button — a thread building something doesn't need an offer to continue a prior conversation.

### Shipped Cards Get Substance (GH #1079)

**Commit `4eda8fa566`**

The 🚀 finish card the CoS posts on completion now includes three parts: what changed, what Alex will notice, and what's left. The card was previously a header without body.

### The Close-Check Marathon (GH #1063-#1084)

**Commits `2974329b26`, `ab44e0b5c8`, `19581fb4ed`, `f8e2d2f08e`, `59fe0b0ca2`, `34f95d5948`, `d9d1f14077`, `b6df0dc9b1`, `7173b96066`, `ae0f97d7ac`, `5de1633999`, `b139840a7a`, `537dda5c32`, `857531fe3a`, `3b9122b4b4`, `11daf80d33`, `755df49612`, `f457b3f9e9`, `74d982de87`**

Nineteen commits across issues #1063-#1084 tightened the close-check logic into a precise behavioral contract:

- **Overlap holds** (#1063): wait only on a leader mid-turn, match owned issues only, expire rather than hold indefinitely
- **Stalled roster threads** (#1072): come back around to the CoS on the next sweep instead of sitting silent
- **Pending wake close check** (#1074): a thread left only with a pending wake gets the close check; the offer lists its pending wakes
- **Keep-going turn** (#1075): a logged turn whose leftovers nothing carries gets one keep-going turn rather than stalling
- **Stop scope** (#1076): a Stop covers only turns that started before it — not concurrent turns that started later
- **Restart resume** (#1078): a restart resume quotes the cut turn's task, or Alex's last message when there's no task
- **Rocket finish card** (#1069): the CoS reports a finish as a 🚀 card always; `b139840a7a` ships it, `537dda5c32` makes it survive review supersession
- **#chief-of-staff replies** (#1067): every answer to Alex's message replies directly to it, even immediately after it
- **Answered-elsewhere** (#1080): a thread whose question was answered in another channel gets its close check when the CoS turn ends
- **Leave thread tick** (#1081): a Leave thread pick whose turn never closed the thread gets closed by the minute tick
- **Buried-card nudge** (#1082): hourly reply-nudge on a buried question card, quiet midnight-8am unless Alex is up
- **Overnight nudge** (#1083): the overnight nudge checks `jfdi active` first — if Alex is up anywhere, it skips
- **Close card gate** (#1084): no close card until an open-items result is saved and posted; a clean check answered in one plain line counts as posted; a relay the turn already read no longer skips its review; the minute backstop reviews any finished turn that got no review

A contract precise enough to test exhaustively surfaces many small gaps at once, and closing them all in one session is more reliable than spacing them out across weeks.

## Reflection: Week 52

### The Mistakes We Made

#### 1. The Inbox Stop Hook Added and Reverted Same Day

**Cost:** The inbox stop hook blocked the turn at Stop to read late relay messages, adding complexity to the stop path.\
**Fix:** `953e5dfce7` — reverted. Late relays naturally wait about 10 seconds for the next turn; the hook solved a problem that didn't need solving at the stop layer.\
**Lesson Learned:** When timing behavior already exists and works, verify what the system already does before writing a gate around it.

One dense focused session cleared the entire backlog by treating close-check as a single system to exhaustively spec, not a collection of individual bugs.
