## Reflection: Week 24

---

### The Mistakes We Made

#### 1. Six Laravel Tasks That Never Ran

**Cost:** Six scheduled tasks existed in the Laravel codebase but never executed because the container's Laravel scheduler wasn't active. These tasks were silently assumed to be running — any behavior they were supposed to drive was simply absent.\
**Fix:** `1577413aa` - Migrated all six to Bree jobs where they actually execute\
**Lesson Learned:** Tasks that exist in code but don't execute are worse than missing tasks — they create false confidence that work is being done.

#### 2. PWYW Double-Processing on Re-Registration

**Cost:** When a registration was re-processed (e.g., after a webhook retry), the existing registration's status was being overwritten, potentially reverting approved registrations to pending.\
**Fix:** `b4a60b7d5` - Preserve status on existing registrations during re-processing\
**Lesson Learned:** Idempotency means "same result on repeat" — not "process again from scratch." Status fields need explicit preservation logic.

#### 3. Quote Attributions in Meeting Notes

**Cost:** Multiple quotes from the 20th anniversary kickoff were attributed to Alex when they were Adam's. Three separate commits were needed to correct attribution across different sections.\
**Fix:** `469abd1a5`, `882d60fe1`, `e0e1e72ef` - Corrected all attributions\
**Lesson Learned:** Transcript-based attribution is unreliable for multi-speaker meetings. Always verify quote ownership against the speaker who introduced the idea, not the one who repeated it.
