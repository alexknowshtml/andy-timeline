## Day 382: The CoS Architecture Overhaul (October 18, 2026)

### Mid-Turn Relay: Messages Find a Running Thread (GH #1061)

**Commits `bf37664b2e`, `acf4a10c3f`, `7ce82719e5`, `4f531a0c62`, `a5a666e628`, `2ce6155f13`, `e1b245e3e5`, `950be5318e`**

You rewired how cross-thread messages reach a busy CoS. Previously, a message for the CoS while Claude was mid-turn in another thread waited in a queue. Now it's delivered directly into the running thread's inbox and read at the next tool call. The host-bridge (`bf37664b2e`) posts into a thread's running Claude inbox; every thread now launches with `crossSessionInbound=accept` by default.

Visual tracking: a relay post gets ✅ once read, 👀 when it becomes the next turn. When a relay arrives mid-turn, the CoS gets context on how to rank it against the current work. The mid-turn notice posts as the CoS persona, anchored below the relay post it describes. `cos:relay --pending` lists a thread's waiting relays; `--withdraw` removes them. The inbox stop hook was added (`587a113e26`), tested, and same-day reverted (`953e5dfce7`) — late relays naturally wait ~10 seconds for the next turn, making the stop block unnecessary.

### Set-Aside Replaces Parked (GH #1057)

**Commits `45e04c7e63`, `e4594de436`, `69073a3d33`, `bbe7253abf`, `46755cfc70`**

The Parked thread concept was retired. Set-aside handles everything Parked did with a cleaner contract: a set-aside card yields to a newer question and comes back once the queue empties. Before the sweep, the thread's accumulated backlog is written to its GitHub issue as a checklist. `cos:defer` lets the CoS ask-card a thread by hand. A reposted set-aside card reads as "asked twice" with a fresh timestamp. `CosParker` was stripped to only closing marks, close path, and legacy-row compatibility.
